About South Sudan & The Poverty Crisis

South Sudan, the world’s youngest nation, gained independence in 2011. Despite its rich culture and natural resources, years of conflict, economic instability, and climate challenges have led to widespread poverty.

Millions of families struggle daily with:

  • Lack of clean and safe drinking water
  • Severe food shortages and malnutrition
  • Limited access to healthcare
  • Low school attendance and poor education systems
  • High unemployment and economic hardship

Children and vulnerable communities are the most affected, limiting opportunities for growth, safety, and long-term stability.
